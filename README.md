# Chronicle (2012
## Movie Synopsis:
The movie is about this bunch of teens that got superpowers from a large crystal under a party, and as the movie went on they develop their powers as much as they can, from controling and holding things with their minds to flying to the sky.
Our main character, Andrew, is a bit depressed because of her dying mom and due to his abusive father and a group of bullies from school. In the movie he was the first to be able to control and stop a baseball from hitting him, implying that the imagination andrew has is better than his friends which was showed throughout the whole movie. The twist of the movie was the abusing and bullying finally caught up with Andrew and with his new found powers, he began to ran havoc to the city.
